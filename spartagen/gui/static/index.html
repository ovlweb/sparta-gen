Sparta base (optional)

Remixes normally sit on a Sparta base. Drop one here: its tempo, bar 1, chords and sections (Intro, Chorus, DunDunDenDen, Awesomeness, Madness, Epicness …) are detected and the remix follows them (“Follow my base”). In “remix” mode the source percussion stays on top of the base; “replace” mutes it.
